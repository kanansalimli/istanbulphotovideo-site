About us

Your own photographer in Istanbul

We are a small photo and video team based on İstiklal Street in Taksim. Every day we shoot on the same rooftops, streets and waterfronts, so we know where the light falls at each hour and which corners stay quiet.

You don't need to know how to pose. We guide you through every shot, pick the locations with you and, on rooftop shoots, pick you up from your hotel. Honeymoons, proposals, families, friends on a trip or a solo traveller: tell us your dates and we plan the shoot around you.

Questions

Before you book

Do I need to bring a dress for the flying dress shoot?

No. The flying dress is part of the rooftop package. Ask us on WhatsApp to see the available colors.

Is the price per person?

No, prices are per session. On the Iconic Locations shoots, group size doesn't change the price. Hotel pickup on rooftop shoots covers up to 3 people.

How do I pay and book?

Message us on WhatsApp with your date. We confirm availability and the final price there.

What if it rains?

Tell us as early as you can and we'll look at another time or day together.

Tell us your dates.

Write on WhatsApp with the shoot you like and when you're in Istanbul. We'll reply with availability and the price.
